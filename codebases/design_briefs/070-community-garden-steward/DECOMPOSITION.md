# 070 — Community garden steward

## Distinct algorithm: separate the preference lattice from the volunteer-duty ledger

The garden steward first records member preferences and prior agreements as two independent ledgers. Preferences describe desired use, availability and accessibility; they do not assign work. Plot constraints are attached to specific plots and observation dates, and consequential soil or safety uncertainties trigger local assessment before a planting plan may depend on them.

Plans are alternatives over shared plots and communal resources, not a single optimizer's allocation. The steward surfaces tradeoffs across access, member goals, shared paths, tools and seasonal care. A reversible trial can resolve a preference conflict only when affected members explicitly agree to its terms. Allocation then advances through proposed, trial, accepted, and declined states; silence never accepts a duty. Seasonal tasks reference their agreement, owner consent, due window, and tool reservations. A broken tool or a member leaving invalidates only dependent tasks, while harvest and care rights transfer through an explicit agreement.

The resumable state keeps membership changes, agreements, plot conditions, trial outcomes, reservations and completion receipts separately versioned. Language negotiates fair options; the host enforces exact reservation overlap, version checks and receipt identity. Missed tasks trigger renegotiation, never automatic exclusion.
