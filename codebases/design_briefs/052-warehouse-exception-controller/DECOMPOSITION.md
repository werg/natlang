# Warehouse exception controller

## Device sequence journal and two inventory balances

Warehouse observations enter an ordered device journal before they can support identity or an adjustment. Duplicate sequences replay without duplicating stock; gaps and unsigned or unverified observations remain quarantined. The stock model preserves lot/serial identity and separates physical on-hand quantity from available quantity, so damage never deletes physical presence.

A late receipt is compared with the exact previous shortage adjustment before any quantity is added. Identity ambiguity generates a discriminating scan; recount independence and custody evidence are separate judgments. The adjustment is a proposal bound to ledger version, not a model-written inventory balance. Actual adjustment and movement receipts are reconciled through a second public operation.

## Verification boundary

The original domain records, five semantic gates, five operational scenarios, and independent finite gate answers remain the contract. Public operations and checkpoints belong to this application. Native loading and source-call inspection do not prove learned execution quality. Effect intent is distinct from an authorized receiver receipt.

The public operation constructs its complete typed carried state on every return, including blocked and no-change outcomes. Only the domain-specific observed change updates its own fields; prior receipts, unrelated history and unresolved external outcomes remain inspectable. Guardrail findings are consumed by the result or the action eligibility they govern.
