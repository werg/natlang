# Qualitative research studio — semantic decomposition

`studyAnalysis.nl` treats the qualitative study as an inquiry-design and interpretation problem, not a record-completion pipeline. It first frames the decision and applicable investigator rules, then builds an `InquiryDecomposition` for each project and enumerates rival explanations before defining sample strata. `freezeSamplingManifest.nl` fixes selected, excluded and nonresponse cases before the codebook is constructed from pilot passages. `constructCodebook.nl` distinguishes descriptive labels from explanatory codes and requires inclusion, exclusion and counterexample examples. `alignTranslationPassages.nl` retains the source passage while `compareIndependentCoders.nl` preserves disagreement and minority interpretations. `searchCounterevidence.nl` searches claims against the declared corpus, and `assessQuotationRisk.nl` assesses direct and indirect identification against the supplied consent events before export.

The output `QualitativeStudyProposal` returns these domain artifacts directly, with unresolved stopping conditions and no claim that missing prior coding, coder records or consent permissions exist. The first-gate qualification fixtures remain exactly as supplied. Export, participant contact, persistence and approved sampling remain external actions; this program produces an auditable proposal only.

The sequence is deliberately evidence-dependent: the sample manifest must precede codebook construction; coding comparison needs both passage and codebook versions; a proposed interpretation cannot support a population claim without counterexample search and bounded sampling. The app does not reuse a universal stage-result or task-graph schema as its domain output.
