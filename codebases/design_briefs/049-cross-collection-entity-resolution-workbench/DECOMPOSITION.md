# Cross-collection entity resolution workbench

Candidate generation is constrained by explicit collection access. Blocking keys and aliases remain visible with their provenance, while samples outside those blocks expose possible recall gaps. Pair judgments separate stable identity evidence from shared names or employers; address coincidence can establish a parent-subsidiary relation without merging them. Recycled identifiers are bound to their validity intervals.

Clusters merge only when all relevant cannot-link and chronology constraints remain consistent. Canonical attributes are resolved field by field by authority and effective time, with aliases and disagreements retained. A later merge reversal propagates a versioned split to dependent records, and a revoked source no longer contributes to canonical values or links. Promotion waits for consumer impact and reversal evidence.
