# Disclosure and redaction review workspace

Disclosure is bounded first by recipient, purpose, document set, authority and time window. Only responsive source spans enter selection, and each stays linked to its exact source hash and locator. Risk is assessed across the assembled fragments because separated facts can combine to reveal a restricted identity. Necessary qualifiers survive redaction; when context cannot be safely retained, the statement is withheld rather than rewritten.

Release requires typed sanitizer receipts for visible content, OCR layers, filenames and metadata, followed by a render and text re-extraction bound to the sanitized output hash. Recipient approval binds the current purpose and exact artifact hash. A changed recipient invalidates delivery authority. Challenges retain a mapping from each released span to its source, redaction basis and approval. Actual dispatch remains an authorized host effect.
