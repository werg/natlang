# 096 — Escape Room Foundry

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: modelExperience, constructPuzzleGraph, writeArtifacts, simulatePlayers, packageOperations. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **modelExperience** — Interpret the creative brief, venue, prop capabilities, audience needs, and approved safety rules. Separate dramatic fiction from actual participant constraints, preserve unrestricted emergency exit and staff intervention, and identify unsupported physical mechanisms before puzzle design. Gate: No real confinement, unsafe mechanism, or inaccessible mandatory path under supplied requirements.
- **constructPuzzleGraph** — Design a dependency graph of puzzles and information reveals, combining parallel and sequential paths deliberately. Give each node a meaningful narrative purpose, avoid circular prerequisites, and ensure every required clue is discoverable before it is needed. Gate: Exact reachability and acyclic prerequisite checks; all mandatory goals have a feasible path.
- **writeArtifacts** — Create in-world documents, dialogue, labels, and clues that support the intended reasoning without relying on designer-only knowledge. Generate plausible player misreadings, preserve thematic coherence, and distinguish fair misdirection from ambiguity that makes the puzzle underdetermined. Gate: Clues are sufficient, internally consistent, and do not require inaccessible external knowledge.
- **simulatePlayers** — Run exact state-space checks and simulated player interpretations, including out-of-order discovery, lost props, repeated actions, and plausible wrong answers. Identify soft locks and confusing clues, then propose targeted repairs rather than adding arbitrary hints everywhere. Gate: Every reachable operational state has a safe exit and a solvable or staff-recoverable route.
- **packageOperations** — Produce the full operating pack: setup, reset, hints, accessibility variants, prop checks, and staff recovery procedures. Verify reset completeness and session isolation, then obtain safety and creative review separately from the mathematical solvability check. Gate: Independent solvability, safe operation, reliable reset, and human enjoyment review all required.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
