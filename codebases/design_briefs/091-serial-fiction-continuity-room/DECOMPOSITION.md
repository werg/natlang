# Serial Fiction Continuity Room

Canon is a versioned graph, not a flattened fact list. Each assertion is typed as world fact, approved plan, character belief, or unreliable narration and points to its manuscript edition and scene. Character knowledge is a separate event ledger: a secret enters a character's knowledge only through an on-page exposure in the active edition. Contradictory drafts remain alternate evidence until an editor promotes one.

A proposal is checked against temporal order, per-character knowledge at scene entry, world rules, motivations and outstanding arc promises. When a twist conflicts, repairs are explored in distinct directions: revise the proposed scene, seed earlier evidence, or request an explicit canon amendment with the full downstream footprint. The author retains the choice. An approved edition reruns only affected continuity descendants, records the diff and checkpoints the new canon version while preserving prior editions.
