# Tender scope comparison design

The resolver compares every bidder against the same requirement revision, but it does not flatten uncertainty into a guessed yes/no cell. A priced allowance remains uncertain, interfaces between trades become named acceptance tasks, and every clarification is bound to the bidder and bid revision it addresses. A later revision that removes an inclusion invalidates the former coverage judgment. Commissioning stays a mandatory functional requirement, so a low total cannot rank as complete when testing and handover are absent.
