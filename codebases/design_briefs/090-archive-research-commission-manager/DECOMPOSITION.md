# 090 — Archive Research Commission Manager

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: frameCommission, planSearch, followEvidence, reconcileFindings, assembleDossier. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **frameCommission** — Interpret the requester’s goal and decompose it into answerable historical questions. Identify what available collections could establish, distinguish factual lookup from interpretive synthesis, and agree a bounded effort and stopping rule before searching indefinitely. Gate: Each subquestion has a plausible evidence path and an explicit inferential limit.
- **planSearch** — Select collections and search strategies using catalog structure, terminology variants, dates, and known access limits. Include negative search paths and record why a collection is relevant rather than relying only on keyword matches. Gate: Search plan distinguishes accessible, inaccessible, unsearched, and exhausted collections.
- **followEvidence** — Read retrieved records in context, extract relevant evidence, and formulate bounded follow-up leads. Distinguish direct evidence from references to missing sources, avoid circular citation chains, and stop low-value leads under the agreed effort budget. Gate: Every lead has a question-specific rationale and source provenance.
- **reconcileFindings** — Compare evidence across sources while preserving chronology, attribution, and disagreement. State what was found and what remains uncertain, and do not turn an unsuccessful search in limited collections into proof that an event never occurred. Gate: Conclusions remain within actual evidence and search coverage.
- **assembleDossier** — Produce an evidence dossier with findings, source references, search history, unresolved leads, and reuse restrictions. Tailor the narrative to the commission while preserving methodological limits and separating the researcher’s interpretation from quoted or recorded facts. Gate: Another researcher can reproduce the searched scope and distinguish evidence from inference.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
