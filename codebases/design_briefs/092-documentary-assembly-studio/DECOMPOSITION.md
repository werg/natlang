# 092 — Documentary Assembly Studio

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: indexMaterial, constructArguments, selectEdits, reviewProduction, freezeAssembly. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **indexMaterial** — Segment interviews and archive descriptions while preserving timecodes, speaker context, and rights. Separate a speaker’s claim from independently established fact and retain qualifiers or questions that affect the meaning of an extract. Gate: Every segment has source timecodes, context, and permitted-use status.
- **constructArguments** — Develop alternative narrative structures around the editorial question and available evidence. Include relevant contradictions and uncertainty, distinguish thematic juxtaposition from causal implication, and avoid inventing connective facts to make the story more dramatic. Gate: Narrative argument remains supportable by the selected material.
- **selectEdits** — Assemble candidate sequences and transitions under exact duration constraints. Inspect how cuts, adjacency, and narration change the apparent meaning of statements, preserving necessary context and identifying misleading implications even when every individual clip is authentic. Gate: No materially misleading quote edit or unsupported causal implication.
- **reviewProduction** — Review the proposed assembly for factual support, rights, attribution, accessibility, and unresolved editorial issues. Distinguish verified claims from attributed testimony and commission targeted fact-checking or clearance tasks before locking the edit. Gate: Required factual and rights gates pass; open issues remain visible.
- **freezeAssembly** — Produce a versioned edit decision list, script, asset manifest, and unresolved-work register. Verify exact timecodes and duration, bind approvals to the assembly, and invalidate affected checks when a source clip or sequence changes. Gate: All deliverables refer to the same approved assembly and licensed source versions.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
