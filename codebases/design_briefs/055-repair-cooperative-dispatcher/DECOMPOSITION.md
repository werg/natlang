# Repair cooperative dispatch design

Dispatch is driven by repair safety and the evidence needed to authorize a specific task. The program first separates observed behavior from suspected cause, then selects only an approved diagnostic procedure. Hazardous or unsupported checks route to specialist intake before any volunteer match or workshop reservation. Matching depends on current credential scope, task consent, availability, and supervision; an expired qualification removes only the task it covers. Work sequencing reserves tools and parts only within approved scope, and a budget or substitution change stops for renewed customer consent. Verification compares the same original symptoms and preserves a recurrence watch when the repair only masks a fault.

A resumed case carries its symptom version, completed task IDs, and pending monitoring. The proposal does not claim that repair work, a referral, or a customer approval has occurred.
