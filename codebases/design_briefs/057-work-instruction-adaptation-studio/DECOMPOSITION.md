# Work instruction adaptation design

The adaptation keeps the approved baseline as its authority and transforms only steps whose local equipment references are unambiguous. Terminology and asset identity are resolved separately because a familiar floor term may still denote multiple controls. Warning translations are checked for retained hazards, conditions, and response requirements before they can enter the variant. Mid-step interruptions are replayed from their actual safe state, and local exceptions preserve every inspection guard they do not explicitly waive. Release also enumerates physical copies at each location so the superseded instruction can be withdrawn.

The output remains a proposed, versioned variant with failed steps, unresolved mappings, and owner-specific withdrawal tasks. It does not assert that a worker was trained or that a printed copy was removed.
