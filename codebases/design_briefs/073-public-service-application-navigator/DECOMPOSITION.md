# 073 — Public Service Application Navigator

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: interpretSituation, identifyPathways, chooseQuestions, assembleApplications, trackOutcomes. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **interpretSituation** — Read the person’s stated situation and supplied documents to identify needs and established facts. Avoid inferring sensitive characteristics, distinguish missing information from ineligibility, and route urgent concerns according to approved service procedures. Gate: Only volunteered or authorized evidence informs the situation model.
- **identifyPathways** — Compare known facts with supplied service descriptions and conditions to identify potentially relevant routes. Preserve multiple plausible pathways, explain uncertainty, and avoid presenting a preliminary match as an official eligibility decision. Gate: Every pathway follows current supplied rules and clearly states unresolved conditions.
- **chooseQuestions** — Select the next question by how much it resolves relevant pathway uncertainty while minimizing burden and sensitivity. Reuse authorized existing evidence, explain why the information is needed, and allow the person to decline or report that it is unknown. Gate: No unnecessary collection or repeated request for already available evidence.
- **assembleApplications** — Convert chosen pathways into document, form, appointment, and submission tasks. Check consistency across forms, preserve the applicant’s own statements, and request review for uncertain answers rather than fabricating details to make a form complete. Gate: Application content is evidence-backed and explicitly confirmed by the applicant where required.
- **trackOutcomes** — Record actual submission and agency responses, distinguish receipt from acceptance or eligibility, and formulate next actions for missing evidence or appeal information under supplied procedures. Preserve the agency’s authority and the applicant’s choices. Gate: No claim of benefit approval without authoritative confirmation.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
