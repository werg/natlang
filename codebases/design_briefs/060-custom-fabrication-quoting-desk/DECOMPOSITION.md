# Custom fabrication quoting design

A quote is formed only after dimensional evidence, selected concept, physical finish acceptance, shop capability, certification scope, material validity, and tooling impact have been checked. Approximate customer measurements remain explicit assumptions until a verified method and tolerance arrive. A rendering cannot stand in for an accepted sample. The route names the exact operation the shop can perform and sends unsupported certified work to a qualified specialist. Price expiry and tooling changes revise both cost basis and production route; no stale number is presented as committed.

The desk returns a provisional or review-required packet and asks for missing scope confirmation. An approved, fully specified, rendered quote can be recorded as an exact issued revision in the quote-decision book. A customer response is bound to that revision and the exact term digest: duplicate responses replay, stale or changed terms stay unresolved, and acceptance is recorded only for a current, approved, non-provisional quote. Issuing or accepting terms does not place a purchase order, reserve shop capacity, or start production.
