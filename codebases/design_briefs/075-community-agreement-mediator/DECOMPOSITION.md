# 075 — Community Agreement Mediator

## Distinct algorithm: preserve accounts and consent states across an agreement's lifecycle

The mediator begins with the exact prior agreement and treats each participant's account as a separate source, not a vote to rewrite contested history. It maps positions and supporting reasons to individual terms, preserving agreed terms beside disputed terms. Options are generated only from those interests and the supplied boundary; legal rights, immediate safety and professional questions are routed out of scope instead of negotiated by the model.

A trial is a state machine with named participants, bounded terms, start/end, review trigger, exit path and independent outcome reports. Acceptance is participant-specific; a nonresponse remains pending. A successful outcome for one person does not become a unanimous success. Withdrawal immediately follows the agreed exit process and prevents further reliance on that participant's consent. The append-only checkpoint keeps prior agreement, competing accounts, current acceptance, trial observations and changed terms, so the next session can resume without treating disputed recollection as fact.
