# Donation redistribution coordinator

Only donor-confirmed quantities enter allocatable stock. Recipient needs are grounded in stated essentials, timing, accessibility, storage, and consent, with no inferred circumstances. Bundles are assessed as complete combinations against the recipient's intended use; substitutions must be acceptable to that recipient and storage constraints can require a split delivery.

Allocation follows the supplied fairness rules against confirmed stock and transport capacity, retaining unmet needs and explaining tradeoffs. Collection-day quantity changes recompute only affected reservations. Delivery plans recheck availability and consent, limit donor-visible information, and treat attempts, acceptance, and decline as separate states. Recalls and handling restrictions quarantine only the affected lot/quantity under the supplied safety policy. Nothing is dispatched by the proposal.
