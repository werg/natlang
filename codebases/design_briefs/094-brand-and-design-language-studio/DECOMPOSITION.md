# 094 — Brand And Design Language Studio

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: recoverBrandConstraints, generateDirections, constructSystem, applyChannels, reviewCoherence. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **recoverBrandConstraints** — Translate the client brief, product facts, and existing assets into desired associations and hard constraints. Separate aspirational positioning from demonstrable capability, identify legal or rights review needs, and preserve audience guidance without inferring personal traits. Gate: Product claims and prohibitions have authoritative sources.
- **generateDirections** — Develop materially different creative directions with coherent naming, tone, and message logic. Explain what each direction emphasizes and excludes, avoid cosmetic variants of one idea, and keep unsupported product promises outside the creative system. Gate: Directions satisfy factual constraints and are meaningfully distinct.
- **constructSystem** — Turn the selected direction into reusable principles and examples across likely contexts. Define how the system behaves in error messages, sensitive situations, and ordinary promotion, preserving consistency without requiring every asset to sound identical. Gate: Rules are operationally usable and examples do not contradict them.
- **applyChannels** — Generate channel-specific assets that serve actual user tasks while respecting length, format, and factual constraints. Adapt emphasis and tone to context, preserve required disclosures, and avoid treating a slogan as a substitute for useful product information. Gate: Each asset is useful in its channel and consistent with the approved system.
- **reviewCoherence** — Compare the asset collection for conceptual, terminological, and factual coherence. Separate hard defects from subjective creative preference, obtain client review of meaningful alternatives, and version the system and dependent assets together. Gate: No unsupported claim or unresolved hard inconsistency; originality receives separate human evaluation.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
