# Adaptive reuse feasibility design

The studio preserves historic drawings alongside recent surveys and resolves each conflict only within the area and method a qualified survey actually covers. A new intended use reopens the constraints and investigations that depend on it while leaving unrelated approvals intact. Structural assumptions gate only the concepts that rely on them and trigger a qualified review. Cost comparisons expose enabling works omitted from an estimate, and phased public opening is checked against temporary accessible routes and active construction conflicts.

The result compares options with explicit contingencies and missing scope. It does not treat a study assumption as structural approval or a budget subtotal as an all-in project cost.
