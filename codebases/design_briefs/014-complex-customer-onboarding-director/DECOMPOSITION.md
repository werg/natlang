# Complex Customer Onboarding Director

Onboarding begins with outcomes that have a named customer owner, measurable acceptance, and explicit exclusions. Environment compatibility is an evidence map by system, identity behavior, security boundary and interface; sample success is not production proof. Departing owner handoff is independently gated by successor authority and credential revocation.

The work plan is a prerequisite graph whose shared prerequisites appear once and whose pilots precede broad imports. Production-sample mismatches create limited, privacy-approved validation before bulk work. Progress is computed per task completion contract from system events; attendance and attempted imports remain different from demonstrated capability and reconciled migration. Replanning retains accepted tasks, updates only failed dependents, and requires customer approval for scope changes. Contract milestones and internal target dates remain separate lanes. No production credential or effect appears in model state.


## Review completion and blocked-state construction (2026-10-26)

Blocked discovery and executable onboarding both return one complete typed result. Plan, progress, and replan start as null; ownership and contractual-date guardrails remain durable on blocked paths. The owner transfer is evaluated once and its actual result flows into the checkpoint.
