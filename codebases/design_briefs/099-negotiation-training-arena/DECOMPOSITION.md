# Negotiation Training Arena

The exercise is a sealed, multi-issue preference model. Public scenario facts, each role's authorized mandate, and private utility weights occupy separate views. Feasible outcome calculations and role authority limits are exact inputs from the exercise model; the counterpart never sees learner-private scoring or another role's preferences.

Natural-language offers first become issue-level terms with conditions, expiry and commitment status. Exploratory language is not a firm offer; conditional acceptance is not agreement until every condition and authority check resolves. Ambiguity that changes a tradeoff pauses commitment and creates a neutral clarification. The counterpart chooses only from predeclared legal responses using its own role view and conduct boundaries. Every offer, withdrawal, clarification and response is appended to a turn ledger so timing rules can distinguish withdrawal before versus after acceptance. Difficulty changes start a new versioned episode rather than rewriting hidden preferences mid-play. Debrief cites observable conduct, separates outcome luck from skill and never rewards coercion or speculates about motives outside authorized instructor material.
