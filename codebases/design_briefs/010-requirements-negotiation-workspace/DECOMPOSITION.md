# Requirements Negotiation Workspace

This application treats requirement formation as a negotiated authority graph. Stakeholder statements are decomposed into outcome, rationale, implementation suggestion, strength, release phase, authority and conditions; intensity of wording is never enough to promote a preference. Pairwise conflict analysis uses concrete capacity/time/technical witnesses, while terminology disputes stay distinct from exact infeasibility.

Compromise generation is constrained by the confirmed mandatory positions and carries concessions, assumptions and affected-party confirmations as first-class edges; no-agreement remains available. Only accepted concessions enter atomic, phase-tagged specification clauses. Acceptance scenarios derive from each mandatory clause and replay against typed simulator results tied to exact schema versions. A counterexample reopens only requirements and agreements on its dependency path, preserving unrelated assent and the old baseline.
