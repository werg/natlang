# 083 — Accessible Narrative Production Desk

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: modelNarrativeInformation, planChannels, writeAccessibleText, validateSynchronization, packageAccessibleMedia. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **modelNarrativeInformation** — Read the script and approved scene metadata to identify dialogue, sound, visual information, and narrative reveals. Distinguish essential understanding from decorative detail and preserve what the audience is meant to know at each moment. Gate: No information is invented beyond supplied media evidence or revealed prematurely.
- **planChannels** — Allocate information across captions, descriptions, transcripts, and supplementary explanations. Identify timing gaps and channel conflicts, prioritize essential meaning, and avoid duplicating dialogue in a way that obscures other necessary information. Gate: Channel plan preserves essential information within actual timing and capacity constraints.
- **writeAccessibleText** — Formulate concise, natural accessible text appropriate to the channel and supplied style guidance. Describe observable actions rather than inferred motives, identify speakers or meaningful sounds where needed, and preserve narrative tone without adding unsupported interpretation. Gate: Text is accurate, appropriately timed, and does not spoil future information.
- **validateSynchronization** — Run exact timing, overlap, reading-rate, and format checks, then review narrative completeness and usability. Inspect transitions and dense scenes where local compliance may still produce an inaccessible overall experience. Gate: Exact synchronization passes and essential information remains understandable across channels.
- **packageAccessibleMedia** — Assemble coordinated format-specific files with stable segment IDs and the correct source revision. Verify player or delivery compatibility, preserve reviewer notes, and update only affected segments when the underlying edit changes. Gate: All deliverables align to the same approved media timeline and version.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
