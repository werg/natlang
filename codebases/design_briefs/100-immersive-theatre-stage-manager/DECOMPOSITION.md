# 100 — Immersive Theatre Stage Manager

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: modelDramaturgy, modelProduction, selectSceneVariants, coordinatePerformance, recoverContinuity. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **modelDramaturgy** — Read the script and production notes to identify scene goals, knowledge reveals, dependencies, and branching choices. Distinguish fixed dramatic obligations from improvisable material and preserve what each character and audience group can know at that point. Gate: Scene transitions respect knowledge and narrative prerequisites.
- **modelProduction** — Reconcile cast availability, venue constraints, props, accessibility arrangements, and participant boundaries. Identify scenes requiring qualified stage control or specific resources, and keep physical-safety instructions outside improvisational model authority. Gate: Required resources and consent conditions are explicit and current.
- **selectSceneVariants** — Select or generate approved-style variants that fit audience choices and current resources. Preserve the dramatic beat, character knowledge, and consent boundaries; avoid replacing a missing prop with an unsafe physical action or unsupported audience involvement. Gate: Variant remains narratively coherent and operationally permitted.
- **coordinatePerformance** — Build an exact cue and movement plan using approved production constraints, then provide role-specific briefings. Confirm critical acknowledgments, preserve stage-manager control, and distinguish a proposed cue from an executed cue in the live state. Gate: No conflicting resource use, impossible timing, or unauthorized safety-critical cue.
- **recoverContinuity** — When cast, props, or audience paths change, propose bounded in-character recovery material and feasible scene adjustments. Preserve revealed facts and participant boundaries, obtain stage-manager approval, and log the actual path for subsequent scenes and post-show reset. Gate: Recovery preserves safety, consent, timing feasibility, and narrative continuity.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
