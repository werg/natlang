# Incident recovery orchestrator

Impact is stated from alerts, reports, and telemetry, with unobserved functions labeled unknown. Several causal hypotheses remain provisional until a diagnostic distinguishes them. Diagnostic operations must be explicitly allowlisted and fit a declared scope and resource budget; intrusive checks wait for commander approval.

Recovery steps come from a named runbook version and require fresh state and authority checks immediately before execution. A timed-out action is reconciled by its exact operation ID before retrying, and a commander change requires explicit transfer of approval. Closure depends on representative user outcomes over the supplied observation window, not green infrastructure metrics. Data inconsistency, residual impact, and recurrence keep the incident open. This source creates a recovery proposal; it does not execute it.
