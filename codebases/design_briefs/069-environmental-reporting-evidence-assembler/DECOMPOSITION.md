# 069 — Environmental reporting evidence assembler

## Distinct algorithm: close the boundary ledger before reconciling physical activity

This assembler builds a report from a boundary-first ledger. It freezes the approved ownership/operational-control boundary and effective period, then maps each activity to source measurements without prematurely converting units or aggregating. A physical event has one identity even when an internal meter and contractor invoice both describe it. The program therefore reconciles activity identity before allocation, distinguishes responsibility transfer from duplicate measurement, and only then allocates shared quantities under the explicit boundary rule using exact arithmetic.

Each claim is checked against measured outcomes at the claim's actual scope. Initiative completion does not prove environmental benefit; estimates retain their method and uncertainty. Boundary changes create a separate restatement bridge so a changed total is not misreported as operational improvement. The frozen edition retains exact schedules, methods, boundary version, claim-to-measure links, unresolved gaps and reviewer binding. A restart can regenerate the same report edition and show later corrections as a new version.

Language interprets supplied boundary terms and qualifies narrative. Exact units, periods, deduplication keys, allocations, and totals are exact service responsibilities. No evidence is silently balanced to produce a favorable number.
