# Technical debt campaign planner

Reports are grouped by observed workflow event and retain frequency basis, severity, team, and evidence. A rare but severe workflow remains visible. Proposed causes stay hypotheses until disconfirming checks separate technical mechanisms from staffing or process explanations.

Work packages carry finite behavior changes, non-goals, owners, and acceptance evidence. Cleanup first discovers undocumented consumers; cross-team packages state interface ownership, and discovery that expands the change resets the scope baseline. The campaign sequences feasible dependency waves under stated capacity and delivers useful intermediate improvements. Outcome checks retain exposure and workload denominators, and a workaround cannot retire until its runbooks and alerts have reviewed replacements. Package completion alone is not an improvement claim.
