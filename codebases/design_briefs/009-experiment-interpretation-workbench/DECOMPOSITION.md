# Experiment interpretation workbench — semantic decomposition

`interpretExperiment.nl` consumes an experiment question and evidence bundle. It frames the named decision, selects applicable rules, and orders each review's plan amendments by their effective time. `orderPlanAmendments.nl` separates prospective criteria from retrospective choices and preserves unresolved authorization or temporal ambiguity. Only after that reconstruction does `checkMeasurementComparability.nl` compare event definitions, timestamp semantics, schema versions and missingness across the design timeline. An affected window is marked invalid or limited before approved output is interpreted.

`checkEstimandAndSearchHistory.nl` binds each approved result to its declared assignment unit, observation unit, population, outcome, uncertainty, multiplicity method and subgroup search history. It reviews supplied computations; the program does not recompute estimates. `seekContraryExperience.nl` then looks for dissatisfied successes and satisfied failures against each approved output, without elevating anecdotes into causal evidence. `deriveFollowupQuestions.nl` proposes confirmatory or exploratory work only where the bundle supplies an eligible cohort, time window, allowed operation and completion test; missing design elements become questions.

The root returns design timelines, metric audits, estimand checks, counterexperience and follow-up questions directly. It does not invent a prior-meeting revision, statistical result, holdout, exposure denominator, or completed production action. The three exact first-gate fixtures are unchanged. The public operation accepts an `ExperimentReviewCheckpoint` and preserves interpretation editions, exact analysis identities and stale-result history. A changed design or measurement reopens only dependent analysis IDs; identical output replay is a no-op. The checkpoint does not claim approval, recalculation, or new data collection.

Review judgments are nested by estimand rather than flattened into one status. Measurement review returns separate event-definition and missingness findings for each affected window; estimand review binds assignment, observation, population, outcome and approval provenance before subgroup-search review; qualitative counterexperience is then gathered against that exact checked output. Each parent's typed result is consumed by the next judgment and by the final prohibited-pool or follow-up fields.
