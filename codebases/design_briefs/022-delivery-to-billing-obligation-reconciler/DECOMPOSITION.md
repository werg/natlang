# Delivery-to-billing obligation reconciler

## Obligation clocks and earned entitlement

The application maintains contract identity separately from project labels, and earned entitlement separately from invoiced consumption. Effective amendments create schedule intervals; delivery events alone do not activate an acceptance trigger. Every phase follows its own condition and customer authority path. A future rate amendment leaves prior earned amounts under their original schedule.

The main operation derives a customer packet from supported unconsumed entitlement. A second operation reconciles actual invoice/void outcomes and updates consumed capacity only on authoritative receipts. An acknowledgment, local void request, or renamed project milestone cannot alter entitlement. Missing contractual clocks, rates, caps or respondent authority create scoped questions rather than inferred billing rights.

Packet preparation returns the complete customer packet and its updated book, even when no slice is releasable. Outcome reconciliation returns the unchanged book on discrepancy or unknown status and enumerates exactly which consumption entries changed, what remains pending, and the customer-facing explanation.

## Verification boundary

The original domain records, five semantic gates, five operational scenarios, and independent finite gate answers remain the contract. Public operations and checkpoints belong to this application. Native loading and source-call inspection do not prove learned execution quality. Effect intent is distinct from an authorized receiver receipt.
