# Culinary Concept Development Kitchen

Menus are ingredient lineage networks. Every dish variant expands to verified ingredient lots, process controls, allergen facts and recipe revisions; a dish name never proves dietary safety. Mandatory restrictions and preferences remain different kinds of requirement. A supplier substitution invalidates only recipes and disclosures depending on that ingredient.

Menu design balances service pacing, variety and dietary coverage across components shared between dishes. Shared mise-en-place is costed and scheduled once, yet linked to every dependent plate. Exact rational recipe scaling, yield, waste and money arithmetic is delegated to the deterministic kitchen ledger, while NatLang compares culinary intent and constraint fit. A line schedule must fit shared equipment capacity at exact intervals. Tasting notes are partitioned into repeatable defects and subjective preference; a sauce revision fans out to all dishes, allergen cards and service plans. Allergen-free claims require venue-specific controls and qualified approval, never a recipe-only inference.
