# Patient Experience Improvement Laboratory

## Administrative mechanism study with consent lineage

The study first separates administrative experience from clinical concerns and routes the latter through the supplied clinical process without interpreting them. Patient-scoped journey linkage precedes deduplication: repeated reports of one episode are not independent experiences. Alternative mechanisms and negative cases determine the next observation to collect.

A measured-trial branch compares the observation frame, channel availability, denominator and downstream workload before interpreting a rate. Severe minority experiences remain visible alongside aggregate findings. Consent withdrawal is an independent early branch following attributable descendants. Study state preserves hypotheses, actual implementation receipts, outcome artifacts, counterevidence and removal obligations without claiming clinical benefit.

## Verification boundary

The original domain records, five semantic gates, five operational scenarios, and independent finite gate answers remain the contract. Public operations and checkpoints belong to this application. Native loading and source-call inspection do not prove learned execution quality. Effect intent is distinct from an authorized receiver receipt.
