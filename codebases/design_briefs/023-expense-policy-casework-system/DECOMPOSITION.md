# Expense policy casework system

## Immutable claim history and line-specific appeal

The application is a casework conversation around a receipt lineage. It opens a new claim, resumes a targeted evidence request, or reconsiders a previous decision only for new appeal evidence. The original claim remains immutable; an appeal does not create another reimbursement identity.

Each receipt is segmented into nights, companions, tax, tips and fees before purpose is interpreted. Business purpose uses stated facts and explicit allocation rules, not merchant stereotypes. Effective policy and scoped exceptions are evaluated per line. Questions are grouped by decision impact and minimized for privacy. A separate approval-authority branch excludes a manager who is also the claimant. The resulting case file records paid, rejected and held portions, independent review, and actual reimbursement receipts.

## Verification boundary

The original domain records, five semantic gates, five operational scenarios, and independent finite gate answers remain the contract. Public operations and checkpoints belong to this application. Native loading and source-call inspection do not prove learned execution quality. Effect intent is distinct from an authorized receiver receipt.
