# Handover continuity manager

## A portfolio of promises that can outlive access

The application separates completion of promised work from acceptance of responsibility. It first interprets a departure/access event and selects a minimal authorized evidence capture. Promise episodes retain conditional activation and unknown dates; actual outcome/acceptance evidence determines remaining work.

Dependency recovery is per open obligation. It distinguishes knowledge, access, approval and external relationships, retains cyclic handoffs, and never places credential values in state. Successor replies are a separate public operation that partitions accepted scope from declined or unanswered scope. An introduction is only delivered on an actual receipt. The handover can close with open work when every immediate obligation has an accepting owner and a confirmed next checkpoint.

## Verification boundary

The original domain records, five semantic gates, five operational scenarios, and independent finite gate answers remain the contract. Public operations and checkpoints belong to this application. Native loading and source-call inspection do not prove learned execution quality. Effect intent is distinct from an authorized receiver receipt.

The public operation constructs its complete typed carried state on every return, including blocked and no-change outcomes. Only the domain-specific observed change updates its own fields; prior receipts, unrelated history and unresolved external outcomes remain inspectable. Guardrail findings are consumed by the result or the action eligibility they govern.
