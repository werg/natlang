# Project-based learning director

The entrypoint begins with goal demonstrations and only then invents a collaborative project setting. It gives each learner a substantive, attributable contribution, and treats material substitution as an equivalence question against the same learning goal. Milestones are ordered from prerequisite IDs and checked against individual workload; a missed milestone can redistribute logistics but cannot erase required personal evidence.

Scaffolds target the observed obstacle without writing the assessable artifact. Unclear group authorship triggers individual demonstrations. A late complication is rehearsed against remaining time, resources, dependencies, and mandatory goal coverage. A creative solution is judged by the competency evidence it demonstrates rather than resemblance to a sample. The checkpoint preserves completed work and outstanding individual evidence for a later invocation. All consequential schedule or assessment changes remain proposals for an accountable teacher.
