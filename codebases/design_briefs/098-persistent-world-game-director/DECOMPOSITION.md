# Persistent World Game Director

The application separates player utterance, proposed action, exact world execution, and character belief. Intent interpretation may produce only a legal action candidate with clarification questions; it never advances state. Each character view is assembled from witnessed events, permitted messages, durable memory and approved facts known to that character. Global world truth and private secrets remain outside the player-facing view.

An action is committed only through the exact simulator receipt bound to its idempotency identity and world revision. Concurrent spends are serialized by the authoritative simulator; failed or ambiguous receipts require readback, never narrative invention or blind retry. Belief updates derive only from actual information channels after execution. Participant boundary changes apply immediately to future content branches. World-rule changes are explicit migrations at a stable checkpoint and preserve historical outcomes, replay references and moderation state.
