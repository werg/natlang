# Living evidence map

A living review freezes its question, eligibility policy, corpus and cutoff before screening. Study reports are linked to one underlying study only where version provenance supports that identity, preventing a preprint and journal article from being double-counted. Extraction retains original outcome units, population, design, follow-up, limitations and exact source locators. Abstract-only access remains a limitation rather than a basis for inferred results.

Comparability is assessed before claim construction; different units require an approved conversion. Claims retain supporting and opposing reports, applicability and evidence lineage. Retractions invalidate only dependent claims and notify owners. Eligibility changes create a new screen version and preserve the prior decisions. This map organizes supplied evidence; it does not fabricate study retrieval or infer unseen results.
