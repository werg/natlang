# 084 — Cross Cultural Training Adaptation Studio

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: recoverLearningMechanism, assessContextFit, generateAlternatives, replayLearning, releaseTraining. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **recoverLearningMechanism** — Analyze the original training to identify the skill exercised by each scenario and the elements that merely provide context. Separate essential decision structure from names, settings, idioms, or assumptions that may be adapted without changing the learning objective. Gate: Every scenario has an explicit instructional mechanism before adaptation.
- **assessContextFit** — Compare scenario elements with supplied audience guidance and reviewer input. Identify unfamiliar references, inappropriate assumptions, or practical mismatches without inferring cultural traits from nationality, ethnicity, or other sensitive attributes. Gate: Adaptation rationale is evidence-based and audience-specific, not stereotyped.
- **generateAlternatives** — Invent alternative settings, examples, and interactions that remain plausible for the supplied context. Preserve the same relevant ambiguity, constraints, and skill demand, and avoid making the adapted task easier or changing the intended lesson unintentionally. Gate: Instructional equivalence and contextual plausibility are separately reviewed.
- **replayLearning** — Compare original and adapted scenarios through representative correct, incorrect, and borderline responses. Detect shifts in difficulty or meaning, inspect whether examples imply unsupported norms, and revise only the elements responsible for the drift. Gate: The same competency distinctions remain observable in both versions.
- **releaseTraining** — Assemble the adapted program with facilitator notes, terminology, and documented limits of applicability. Obtain audience and instructional review, version the relationship to the source program, and avoid presenting one adaptation as universal for an entire culture. Gate: Approved audience scope and preserved learning objectives are explicit.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
