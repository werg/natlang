# 084 — Cross Cultural Training Adaptation Studio

## Distinct algorithm: preserve the competency decision while replacing context-specific cues

The studio decomposes a training scenario into the target competency, required decision, decision complexity, and incidental analogy or setting. It compares the incidental elements with supplied audience guidance and reviewer observations, preserving disagreements about audience assumptions rather than generalizing about a group. If an analogy is unfamiliar but the competency is essential, it changes the analogy while holding the learning objective fixed.

Every candidate adaptation is replayed against the original decision demands. If the new version is easier, it restores the required ambiguity, consequence, and tradeoff without reinstating an unsuitable stereotype or analogy. Tests record where learners succeed or fail and preserve that finding as a versioned result. A source curriculum update invalidates only adaptations whose competency mechanism changed; adaptation releases are immutable and require reviewer approval.
