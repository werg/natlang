# 089 — Collection Rights And Consent Steward

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: extractPermissions, interpretUse, assessCompatibility, requestClearance, maintainUseRegister. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **extractPermissions** — Read donation agreements, licenses, consent statements, and amendments to identify permissions and restrictions. Distinguish ownership of a physical object from rights to reproduce or publish it, and preserve missing or conflicting documentation as uncertainty. Gate: Absence of a restriction record is not treated as permission.
- **interpretUse** — Translate the proposed exhibition, publication, or derivative use into concrete acts and distribution scope. Identify cropping, translation, online access, commercial use, and duration where relevant, rather than treating all publication as one undifferentiated permission. Gate: Proposed use is specific enough to compare with the governing records.
- **assessCompatibility** — Compare each proposed act with supplied rights and consent conditions, including interactions among multiple rightsholders. Preserve scoped exceptions and refer ambiguous legal interpretation to the rights officer rather than selecting the most permissive reading. Gate: Every allowed use has positive authority and all applicable conditions are satisfied.
- **requestClearance** — Form a precise clearance request describing the actual use, audience, modifications, and duration. Track responses and conditions without treating informal enthusiasm as a license, and maintain restricted access to private donor or participant information. Gate: Clearance closes a gap only with appropriate documented authority.
- **maintainUseRegister** — Record approved uses, required attribution, expiry, and downstream distribution. Recheck conditions before release, schedule removals or renewals where needed, and propagate consent changes to affected derivatives and public surfaces. Gate: Approved use, actual distribution, attribution, and duration remain aligned.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
