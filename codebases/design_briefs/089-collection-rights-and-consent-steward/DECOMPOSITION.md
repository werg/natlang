# 089 — Collection Rights and Consent Steward

## Distinct algorithm: derive permissions for a use by intersecting every item's separate rights record

Ownership of a physical object, copyright, interview consent, reproduction right and online license are separate permission sources. The steward models each proposed use as a tuple of item, channel, transformation, audience, attribution, and duration. A derivative combining several items is permitted only where every contributing item's rights cover that entire use. Permission to print does not imply online permission; a time-limited consent creates an expiry review and removal obligation.

Clearance tasks go to the rightsholder or authorized contact for the specific missing right, not a generic donor. An attribution correction propagates across every affected print, web and promotional asset as a versioned amendment. The use register records current scope, license versions, expiry actions and affected derivatives; it never converts an unresolved rights question into a grant.
