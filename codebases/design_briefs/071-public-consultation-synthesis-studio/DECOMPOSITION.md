# 071 — Public Consultation Synthesis Studio

## Distinct algorithm: preserve scoped arguments while separating participation counts from argument frequency

This program treats a submission as a container that may hold several positions, reasons, factual claims and requested actions. Extraction keeps those scopes attached to the same source; support for one proposal component never erases opposition to another. Template analysis builds relations between copied text and independently added reasons, but does not collapse participant identities or participation counts. A campaign may explain repeated language while every valid participant remains counted.

The synthesis is a bipartite graph from submission-attributed arguments to themes. It retains minority positions and cross-cutting concerns, and labels unresolved disagreement as factual, value-based, or mixed only when the submissions support that distinction. Response options belong to the accountable public body and show investigation or clarification paths alongside policy alternatives; the program cannot choose priorities for officials.

Before report creation, an omission audit traces every extracted argument to a theme, response disposition or explicit out-of-scope reason, and exact participation denominators are reconciled independently of template clusters. Privacy review removes identifying detail from examples unless disclosure is authorized. A challenged report reopens the argument-to-theme coverage graph and emits a versioned report delta rather than rewriting the prior edition.
