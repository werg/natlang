# 074 — Volunteer Deployment Coordinator

## Source-derived procedure

Five domain stages: interpretMission, interpretProfiles, constructTeams, prepareBriefings, replanAssignments. Each stage has a bounded evidence preparation step, field-level decision, typed draft construction, and independent audit. Ten cross-cutting procedures frame scope, select authority, plan dependencies and evidence, reconcile collections, resolve questions, propose bounded repairs, prepare review, compose a recipient-specific view, and assess the impact of source deltas.

## Domain algorithms and gates

- **interpretMission** — Translate the mission brief into concrete tasks, capability requirements, and practical constraints. Distinguish essential qualifications from preferences, identify supervision needs, and preserve safety or safeguarding requirements supplied by the organization. Gate: Requirements are task-based and not inferred from volunteer demographics.
- **interpretProfiles** — Read volunteer profiles and confirmations to distinguish self-reported skills, verified qualifications, availability, and accepted roles. Preserve limitations and preferences, and avoid treating past participation as consent to a new assignment. Gate: Capability and consent are explicit and current.
- **constructTeams** — Build feasible team configurations that collectively cover mission requirements and supervision. Use exact scheduling and capacity checks, explain role assignments, and preserve fair application of supplied assignment principles rather than optimizing convenience alone. Gate: All required capabilities and consent conditions satisfied or the mission remains blocked.
- **prepareBriefings** — Generate role-specific briefings with task boundaries, practical logistics, and escalation routes. Share only necessary participant information, distinguish instructions from optional suggestions, and obtain acknowledgment for consequential responsibilities before deployment. Gate: Briefings preserve approved procedures and contain no unauthorized personal disclosures.
- **replanAssignments** — Process withdrawals and changed mission conditions by identifying exact capability gaps. Propose replacement teams or reduced scope, seek fresh consent for changed roles, and cancel unsafe or incomplete deployments rather than pretending nominal headcount is sufficient. Gate: Replanned teams retain required coverage, supervision, and current consent.

## Action boundary

The natural-language program produces a proposal for accountable review. Authorization, persistence, and external effects are exact host responsibilities. Evidence gaps and conflicts stay visible; no action is inferred from fluent completion.
