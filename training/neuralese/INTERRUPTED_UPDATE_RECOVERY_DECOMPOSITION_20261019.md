# Preserve earned work across an operational correction

The supervisor decides in natural language whether a useful correction warrants
interrupting production. A stopped quantum remains incomplete. The mechanism
below preserves its actual work without granting runtime qualification.

1. Capture the actual bridge and trainer process tree before signaling. Record
   the exact trainer identity, requested operational signal and practical reason.
2. Let the trainer finish its update and save full state. Retain the provider's
   authentic failed result, terminal and complete artifact inventory unchanged.
3. Independently pin the saved checkpoint. Decode it once to verify the actual
   optimizer, RNG, ordinary row bindings, exposure and stage/global counters.
4. Authenticate the existing measured resident capacity, exact runtime/profile,
   initial owner and adopted source snapshots. Inherited capacity is not a new
   measurement. A deferred runtime certificate stays deferred.
5. Reuse the existing dead-owner acknowledgement and historical operation-chain
   verifier. The recovered operation cannot be the final successful handoff; a
   real successor must resume its full state, carry all debt and qualify normally.

These are crisp durability and identity checks, shared by both model lines.
The separate recovery schema never reinterprets legacy successful boundaries.
