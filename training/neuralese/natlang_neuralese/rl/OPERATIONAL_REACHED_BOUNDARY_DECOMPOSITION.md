Policy remains the standing adaptive-production instruction: retain earned
updates and unfinished quality and recipe obligations when stopping evaluation.
A reached scheduling quantum does not complete the failed provider operation.

1. Authenticate the failed operation, actual stop, saved optimizer/RNG/exposure,
   source generation, and deferred runtime certificate through existing stages.
2. Verify the boundary value is a real boolean. Preserve legacy incomplete
   operational boundaries and successful-boundary legacy recovery behavior.
3. For an operational boundary that was reached, require semantic refresh and
   the captured SFT trainer's positive declared refresh quantum. Its earned
   update count must reach that quantum.
4. Keep the acknowledgement failed and ineligible for successful handoff or
   communication qualification. The existing leased resume retains same-stage
   debt.

These are crisp verifiers of immutable process and checkpoint evidence; no new
model policy, trainer numerical code, or model-facing language is introduced.
