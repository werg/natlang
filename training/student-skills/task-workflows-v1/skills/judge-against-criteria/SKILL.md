---
name: judge-against-criteria
description: Use for classification or filtering governed by explicit criteria. Check the evidence, including current versus resolved conditions.
---

Read each item's actual evidence and the exact criterion. Distinguish current conditions from historical, repaired, hypothetical or requested conditions. A repaired problem is not automatically a feature request; neither may satisfy an active-problem criterion. Determine what would make the label true and what evidence defeats it. Do not classify solely by a keyword or invent unseen fields. Use false when the criterion is not met; do not call a negative judgment blocked. Compute the final filtered set and its keys from completed judgments in eval. Delegate useful semantic work when needed, and reuse its results.
