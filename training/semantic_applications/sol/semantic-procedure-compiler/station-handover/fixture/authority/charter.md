ID AUTH. Closed fictional radio-club procedure authority.
[A01] Club Committee CC alone adopts or replaces station safety and custody procedures. An adopted edition applies from its explicit effective day, not publication day.
[A02] Duty coordinator DC can confirm emergency priority and waive the queue prerequisite for that confirmed emergency only. DC cannot waive safety acknowledgment, custody or hazard clearance.
[A03] Operator recollections and habit notes have no adoption force. There are no unlisted policy changes or additional competent waiver grants.
