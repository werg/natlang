[K01] This is an original fictional miniature language and a closed fieldwork exercise. Every corpus transitive clause has exactly two distinct core participants, one agent and one patient, and the event LIFT. The same entity cannot be both event agent and event patient.
[K02] Core nominal order is free in this model. The two nominal expressions and final verb need not put the agent first. A source-licensed role-marked nominal supplies its role; the other core participant takes the complementary event role if that assignment is uniquely licensed.
[K03] A topic is a discourse role, independent of event agent/patient. One entity may be topic and agent, or topic and patient. Two topic/agent contributions for the same entity can coexist; two distinct entities cannot both fill the unique agent slot.
[K04] The candidate domains in D01-D04 are explicitly closed and independent; consider their complete cross-product. These are proposed analyses, not a declaration of the real language. No additional order-based or lexical candidate may be invented to rescue a contradicted combination.
