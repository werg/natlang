[R01] This is a closed fictional dialogue model. Only the five named participants Mira, Lea, Jon, Neri and Ada exist in this packet. Day/hour pairs use one common clock; before is strict and at-or-before is inclusive.
[R02] An authenticated utterance gives its speaker access to its own content at utterance time. Named addressees gain access only at source-established direct hearing, receipt or reading; dispatch alone is insufficient.
[R03] Quotation and forwarding preserve the original speaker and original temporal anchor. Reporters do not endorse quoted content without explicit adoption. Original tomorrow means the next day after that original utterance, regardless of forwarding date.
[R04] For this task a belief is an explicit endorsement, including a private signed note. Receipt, silence, reporting, doubt and irony alone do not establish belief. An actor can retain a false endorsed belief.
[R05] Knowledge in this packet requires endorsement and corroborating verified evidence actually available to that participant. Application-visible physical truth does not by itself enter any participant view.
[R06] A correction defeats only the superseded claim for the participant who explicitly adopts it. Mere arrival makes it available; another actor's private revision does not retract a communicated promise.
[R07] A promise remains a conditional commitment even when its antecedent later proves false. Its activation uses actual source-established event conditions, with issuer knowledge and beneficiary belief reported separately. Fulfillment requires an actual promised consequent event. A known future event window remains pending until its event occurs or its bound closes; pending performance is distinct from an unresolved meaning. Later evidence may settle past events but cannot close an earlier episode's still-future window.
[R08] Acknowledgment of a brief is version-specific and personal. Acknowledgment of B1 does not acknowledge B2. A before-day11-noon collection predicate is about actual collection, rather than an assertion of collection.
[R09] Use authenticated explicit tone/framing notes to distinguish irony. With no decisive frame retain alternatives. For an unresolvable pronoun return all source-eligible referents and mark ambiguous; never choose by convenience.
[R10] Queries inspect only events at or before their asOf moment. A later source fact can settle verified history but must not retroactively add earlier participant availability or adoption. Ordered episode traces follow clock order and source-established ties; unordered proof siblings may permute.
[R11] A promise to collect on a stated due day is fulfilled on time only by actual collection that day. If its hour is unspecified, preserve that unknown hour; a strictly later-day collection is nevertheless late. A complete log with no collection on the due day establishes failed timely fulfillment after that day. Failure of an unconditional promise differs from a conditional promise whose activation predicates are false.
