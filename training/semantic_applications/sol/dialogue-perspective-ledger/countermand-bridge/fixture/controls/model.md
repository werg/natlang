[R01] This is a closed fictional dialogue model with Oren, Sada, Tavi, Noor and Remy. Day/hour pairs use one common clock. Before is strict; at-or-before is inclusive.
[R02] An original speaker accesses their own turn at its authenticated time. Other actors access content only at source-established hearing or actual receipt/read time; dispatch alone is insufficient. Private signed notes remain private.
[R03] Reporting and quotation preserve original speaker, time and target identity. A reporter does not perform the quoted promise or withdrawal and does not endorse it without explicit adoption.
[R04] Belief requires explicit endorsement, including a signed private note. Receipt, silence, doubt and reporting alone do not establish belief. A false claim can remain endorsed until this actor adopts a defeating correction.
[R05] Knowledge qualification requires endorsement of the actual proposition value and corroborating verified evidence actually available to that actor. Application-visible global truth does not enter participant access automatically.
[R06] Withdrawing a promise requires the issuer's communicated retraction of its exact original promise plus explicit adoption by every named beneficiary. Partial adoption preserves the actual adopter set without globally retracting. Final adoption changes the issuer-owned normative commitment, even if the issuer has not learned that adoption occurred. Belief/knowledge of adoption is separately tracked.
[R07] An adopted withdrawal is terminal for that original promise. Later true conditions, deliveries or due boundaries cannot reactivate it. A fresh performed promise has its own lineage and audience. Actual activation, actual fulfillment and actor belief/knowledge are separate readings.
[R08] A future condition window remains pending before occurrence or closure; a claim of occurrence is not an actual event. Personal acknowledgment is specific to actor and brief version. A B2 acknowledgment does not satisfy B3.
[R09] A promise to do an event on a stated day is timely only through an actual event that day. Preserve an unstated hour. Earlier or later events are not on-day fulfillment. A complete negative day log settles absence only after that day closes.
[R10] All original records below are authentic and complete within their stated horizon. Missing actor endorsement is a known absence when no other private/shared note exists. Forwarded text supplies availability rather than endorsement; no outside act or physical occurrence may be invented.
