# Countermand Bridge: connected second Dialogue world

## Changed causal and perspective structure

The first world tests nested original quotation, mistaken private belief and delayed correction.
This world tests actual multi-beneficiary withdrawal across issuer/audience ledgers. An original
conditional two-beneficiary promise receives one adoption, then another after a different receipt.
Only the full adopted revision retracts the issuer's original commitment. A later physical antecedent
becomes true and a related consequent occurs; neither reactivates that terminal lineage. A fresh
promise with a different audience, extra versioned prerequisite and due day separately fulfills.

The same snapshot exposes issuer ignorance of one beneficiary's private adoption. Actual normative
commitment status and the issuer's belief/knowledge remain different fields. A forwarded withdrawal
retains original revision ownership, while a late authenticated adoption summary makes evidence
available and requires a separate explicit endorsement before knowledge can qualify.

## Natural-language stage ownership

1. Intake inventories authenticated original turns, source permissions, complete bounded events and
   receipts; dispatch and actual arrival are separate. Each source record keeps its original identity.
2. Quotation scope preserves the original issuer/time/target of the forwarded withdrawal; the report
   does not become a fresh promise or revision. The typed original span frontier supplies its measure.
3. Endorsement interprets each actual actor's adopted revision IDs, preserving known nonadoption and
   version-specific personal acknowledgment. Receipt alone cannot populate adoption IDs.
4. Revision target selection joins an adopting act's returned IDs to the actual communicated revision
   and its named original promise. It separates an original revision from the adopter's own act.
5. Cross-view withdrawal joins issuer-owned commitment plus every beneficiary's actual adoption/access.
   Partial adoption records that evidence without changing issuer status; final adoption owns the effect.
6. Condition/fulfillment stages judge later actual collection and key events under the appropriate old
   versus fresh due scopes. Terminal retraction survives new facts. Fresh original act identity owns a
   new ledger entry, so recomputation never overwrites the retracted promise.
7. Query proof joins historical status, available evidence and actual endorsements. Exact typed state
   replay applies returned effects; it does not choose which revision or promise those effects mean.

## Exact seams and source qualification

Source bytes, IDs, original quote spans, common-clock comparisons, generated-reference integrity and
application of returned actor-owned deltas are crisp. All target selection, audience adoption, scope,
condition meaning and status policy are numbered NL. Private expected projections stay outside fixture;
a full private typed witness/checker and independent review follow visible source reconstruction.
A source-valid certificate is not an NL execution. No generation or training admission from this draft.

## Added callable target-selection contract

`resolveRetraction/selectRevisionTarget(act: ActReading, decision: EndorsementDecision,
episode: Episode, source: DialogueSource, scoped: ScopedConversation) -> RevisionSelection`
will own the exact interpreted revision lineage. The typed result carries nullable revisionActId,
targetActIds, targetPropositionIds, kind (belief-correction, commitment-retraction, none or unresolved),
communicated:boolean|null, actual source witnesses and unresolved premises.

1. Inventory actual performed correction/retraction candidates in scoped. Keep quoted depictions
   separate from newly performed acts and preserve their original turn identity.
2. Join actual decision.adoptsActIds to those original candidates. An acknowledgment may adopt a
   revision without itself performing the revision. A nonadoption/doubt selects no applied revision.
3. For each selected candidate consume its own supersedesTurnIds and meaning scope. Resolve actual
   source acts for those original turns; a fresh promise remains a different lineage.
4. Judge whether the original revision was communicated under its actual source audience/receipt
   evidence. Private intention cannot acquire an audience from another actor's later report.
5. Separate corrected propositions from commitment-target scope: a collection-time correction is
   not an implicit cancellation. Preserve source-explicit target exclusions.
6. Preserve unresolved competing target candidates rather than choosing by recency or text overlap.
7. Return the actual target selection with nonempty original quotes. Parent exact validation checks
   generated/original ID membership; it does not choose the intended target.
8. Parent consumes this result to join issuer-owned commitments with all actual beneficiary views.
   Last adoption can update normative status while the issuer's knowledge remains absent. The
   returned RevisionDelta applies only to actual owner IDs and history records that exact effect.

The existing first-world source remains separately frozen until its pending full review closes.
This new world and target-selection extension are held pending a full typed certificate, exact
controls and independent source review; the prose sketch above is not an executed trajectory.
