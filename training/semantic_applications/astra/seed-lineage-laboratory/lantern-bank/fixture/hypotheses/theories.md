[H01] Exactly one of D, R and M governs the adult lamp trait for this bank. These are competing hypotheses, not three simultaneously applied rules.
[H02] Under D an adult is bright if its nuclear genotype has at least one A; aa is dark. Cytoplasm does not affect this adult trait.
[H03] Under R only aa adults are bright; AA and Aa are dark. Cytoplasm does not affect this adult trait.
[H04] Under M an adult is bright exactly when its cytoplasm is amber. Nuclear genotype does not affect this adult trait.
[H05] These adult rules do not determine maternal seed-coat color. That separate tissue follows I05.
