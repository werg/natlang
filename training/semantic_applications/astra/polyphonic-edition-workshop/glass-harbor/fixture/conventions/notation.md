[N01] This closed score uses integer pitch ranks, not any external tuning system. Duration glyph I means one tick, II means two ticks, and O means four ticks. Every performed section lasts four ticks.
[N02] Bell sounds its effective written rank unchanged. Reed sounds exactly two ranks above its effective written rank. Apply that instrument offset once after interpreting written accidentals.
[N03] In one voice within one section occurrence, raise sets the current written rank's offset to +1. Natural sets that rank's offset to 0. A bare note uses that rank's current offset, initially 0. Other ranks keep their own settings; raising is a setting, not an accumulating addition.
[N04] Every section occurrence begins with no carried accidental settings and no previous sounded tone. A reprise uses the same chosen written edition but resets each voice's notation state. Each voice's events run consecutively in their written order.
[N05] An attack sounds a new event. A rest consumes its stated duration, has no sounding rank and clears previous tone while preserving accidental settings. A continuation consumes its stated duration without a new attack and keeps the preceding sounding rank; a continuation without a previous sounded tone is unresolved.
[N06] An editorial written-rank shift changes the underlying rank before notation-state interpretation. It does not itself create a new raise/natural sign. A duration correction changes only its duration glyph unless its original instruction explicitly changes another field.
