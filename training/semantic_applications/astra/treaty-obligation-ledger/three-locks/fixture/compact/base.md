[C01] The Three Locks Compact is effective from day0 for Alder and Cedar and from each accepted accession date for a later member. All event times are integer global days.
[N_TOLL] For each transit, its operator port owes its destination lock port a toll of10 credits. The member, reach and event clauses determine applicability.
[N_NOTICE] For each transit, its operator port must send an arrival notice to its destination lock port. Sending this notice once discharges all sources of this same notice duty for that trip.
[N_TRANSLATE] For each transit, its operator port must provide its notice in the destination lock port's language. This translation duty is separate from sending the underlying notice.
[N_PASSAGE] For each transit, its destination lock port must permit passage to its operator port unless a competent effective closure applies. A certified rescue must be permitted despite a flood closure.
[N_RESCUE] A certified rescue transit is exempt from the toll. The ordinary notice and translation duties still apply. Certification by R establishes rescue; an open claim awaiting that certification leaves the exemption unresolved.
[C07] Obligation business identity is (tripId,debtor,beneficiary,action). Actions are toll,notice,translation,passage. Independent clauses for the same identity are alternative justifications, not duplicate obligations.
