[G01] With an empty carrier the robot fits every listed narrow tunnel. Picking up the sealed sample in V costs1 energy and fills the carrier; the robot then cannot traverse any narrow tunnel, in either direction.
[G02] The sealed sample may not be opened, cached, discarded or passed separately through a tunnel. A mission arrival without the sealed sample fails.
[G03] The wide chimney fits the full carrier. If the correct anchor is rigged, ascent V-to-J costs2 energy and uses the fixed rope without consuming another spool. The chimney is not an available descent route from J.
