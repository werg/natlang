# Strategic review, 2026-10-12

Read-only review against the three lodestar pillars: (1) the model family, (2) NatLang and the harness, (3) the
training procedure. Evidence: AGENTS.md; the owner memory records; plans/; git since 2026-09-28 (4,247 commits,
HEAD adc5464b); recipes; the coord log (1,545 messages since 10-05). No code was changed and no job was touched.

**The verdict in one paragraph.** Since 10-10 almost all effort has gone into one thing: making token SFT of Mellum
(and LFM) fast and fed with data. That work is real. Speed went from 45.7 to about 19 s/update, and supervised
tokens went from about 250k to about 90M. But the running Mellum v4 contains **no Neuralese training at all**. Its
recipe has two `neuralese_direct` stages, and their value metric is report-only. Its gates **measure no natlang
program execution**. The NatLang pillar has had **no application commits since 2026-10-10**. Several
vision-defining goals went dormant without a recorded decision: the nested family, the memetic optimiser, S6 and
S7, the harness bench as a Neuralese bench, and LFM QAT. The project is drifting from "Neuralese agents running
natlang programs" toward "a well-engineered SFT pipeline". The main loss is the missing feedback loop: nothing in
the run tells us whether the model gets better at the thing the vision is about.

## 2. Direction check per pillar

**Pillar 1: models.**
- *On track:*
  - The Mellum backbone choice.
  - The identity-initialised direct design (a sound repair of the warm-up collapse, WARMUP_AUDIT_20261011).
  - FP8 expert GEMMs.
  - QAT present in the Mellum loop.
- *Drifting:*
  - Line parity is declared but not real. Pop runs `neuralese-direct-lfm-muon-main-20261012-v2`, not the declared `neuralese-direct-lfm-v3`, with a different stage name, input, span and no QAT. Mellum is on r3 and Pop on r2.
  - Neither line has done anything Neuralese-specific since 10-11.
- *Missing:*
  - The nested family.
  - Any stage that reaches full ternary.
  - A deployment/serving target for Mellum MoE (S8).

**Pillar 2: NatLang and the harness.**
- *On track:*
  - The language and runtime are current, and the skills were updated with each semantic commit (8aba511f, 96601d8b).
  - pluggable.ts is the single selection mechanism.
  - The compilers and nldb are genuine NL-heavy ports.
- *Drifting:*
  - Apps burst 10-07..10-10 and then stopped completely.
  - Most apps are 2–10× more crisp lines than NL lines: notebook, terminal, ide, helpdesk and publisher are over 5×.
  - pi's NL policies are opt-in (`--pure`).
  - The promotion and GEPA policies default to crisp.
- *Missing:*
  - A harness that anyone uses.
  - Specializer output on real apps.
  - Tier 4.
  - Effects-as-data beyond `after()`.

**Pillar 3: training procedure.**
- *On track:*
  - Whole-trajectory rendering.
  - Failed-turn masking.
  - Resumable data handoffs (7d6030c0).
  - The profiler and speed bench.
- *Drifting:*
  - The procedure has become a single long SFT stage with data swapped underneath it (r2 → r3 → r4).
  - The "curriculum" (QAT ramps, the E2E stage) is tied to run progress that is weeks away.
- *Missing:*
  - A task-success eval in the loop.
  - Nested members as objectives.
  - An on-policy/RL stage.
  - Any use of the harness to generate on-policy data.

## 3. Short-sightedness

1. **Neuralese is not trained or evaluated end to end anywhere in the critical path.**
   - The only E2E run is Pop's LFM best225/687 epoch. It is unqualified and ran LionSR without the drift monitor.
   - The program is named after the latent channel, yet its main GPU will spend the next one to two weeks on token SFT.
   - An E2E stage could start from any direct checkpoint now, for example a short E2E arm from v4's current state on Pop or in a DGX slot, and de-risk the defining capability early. Waiting for v4 to finish defers all Neuralese learning signal until about 10-20 or later.
2. **The gate measures the wrong thing.**
   - The 48-probe behaviour gate has these parts:
     - 12 arithmetic probes;
     - 12 tool probes checked only for tool name and argument shape, not executed;
     - 24 probes checked only for stopping and repetition (POP_MAIN_RUN_AUDIT_20261012 l.24–30).
   - It contradicts the owner's breadth-via-natlang-domains rule (10-05): forgetting general ability is fine and is to be reported, not gated. Yet "base minus 0.1" on generic probes is the *hard* criterion.
   - Natlang program success is measured nowhere on Mellum.
   - On LFM the held-task eval exists (bba2f4ca, c072e87b) but does not gate. Its **base scored 0/48**. Under the owner's own rule ("degradation means bug") a 0/48 base is far more likely an eval or rendering bug than a fact about the model. Until that is resolved, no run has a working measure of the target capability.
3. **The harness is a data source, not a bench.**
   - SWE-rebench/OpenHands trajectories replayed in pi become plain SFT rows.
   - The harness-killer-app idea had three parts: the student in the agent role, support delivered as Neuralese, and the companion running live. None of them exists, so the harness provides no on-policy data and no evaluation.
4. **Optimising data plumbing over data breadth.**
   - r3 is concentrated:
     - static-lambda-v2 is 55% of the full rendering's supervised tokens;
     - v13-r2, s72 and campaign-uncovered make up 74% of program-header.
   - The domain-broadening sources are admitted but not in the mix: S1's 1.87M records over 45 families, view-ask and S2.
   - Luna child-action packets are under 0.3% of tokens but a large share of coordination traffic (closure packets, review receipts).
5. **Run length.** Owner rule: state run length in wall-clock time. About 8–11 days for program-header and an unestimated trajectory phase on one run, with no mid-run task eval, means a bug in data or rendering would surface only at the end. Three such bugs surfaced in r3 within one day: 19,921 duplicate ids, cd744222 truncation to 28 of about 3.2k documents, and the generic 16k prompt at serve time (c1de43eb).

## 4. Not radical or principled enough

- **Policy is natural language: violated where it matters most, our own operations.**
  - These decide what is discarded, what trains, what is admitted and what runs, all in crisp Python with no NL or shadow path:
    - storage_retention.py (506 lines);
    - pool approval;
    - the data-admission scripts;
    - the Luna dispatchers;
    - training-gate explanation.
  - The owner explicitly extended the rule to "natlang's own runtime and compiler" (pluggable-hot-paths, 10-09).
  - The project's own operations are the largest natlang program it runs daily, and the best training-data source, yet they are not in natlang.
- **Checkin-is-an-agent, but coordination is a stream of notes.**
  - About 90 agent identities and about 450 messages per day: 1.31M characters, about 1,150 pages.
  - Roughly 40–50% is gatekeeping, review or status. 50 are corrections or retractions, and 60 are "main red" handoffs.
  - The coordination protocol is conventional human-team process run by agents. A natlang design would have each machine expose a status *value*, decisions as data, and closure/verification as exact verifiers, not as requests to the other machine.
- **One implementation per concept: violated in recipes.**
  - There are 119 recipes. 57 reference discarded designs: warm-up, input-map, sketch, the mapped/AR graph and token-preserving foundations. About 40 more are one-off diagnostics.
  - The storage order (2026-10-11) explicitly says to delete obsolete recipes.
  - Pop's running recipe is standalone, not an `extends` of the shared base.
- **Teach in errors / measure live: deferred wholesale.** 64 unmeasured model-facing changes break a rule the owner set specifically to protect small-model behaviour. The teacher windows went to data generation instead.
- **No premature limits / no fixed thresholds** (owner, 10-03: "continuous evaluation + react/improve, no numeric gates"). The direct stage still runs on numeric KL and behaviour thresholds, while the capability that matters has no metric at all.
- **Bolder moves warranted:**
  - Train E2E Neuralese and nested members *concurrently* with SFT, as extra objectives in the same pass. Do not run them as sequential stages after it. The owner asked for "QAT inside every stage" and "members in every stage" for this reason.
  - Make the harness the generator of on-policy trajectories: the student runs natlang programs, verifiers grade the runs, and the results feed SFT, DPO and RL.

## 5. Optimization opportunities

- **Compute.**
  - v4 is 1 epoch of program-header at about 31k updates. Rendering the headers is about 53% of Mellum's supervised tokens but **766M total tokens versus 43M supervised**: about 5.6% supervised.
  - Packing, shorter header context, or prefix-shared batching (many rows share program headers) could cut the cost per supervised token substantially. This is worth more than the next kernel round.
  - Run one short E2E arm in parallel instead of serially.
- **Data.**
  - Fix r3 → r4 before the next handoff: duplicate ids, `supersedes`, and the trajectory truncation.
  - Bring in S1, view-ask and SWE D13 as natlang domains.
  - Stop spending coordination on sub-0.3% packets.
- **Process and coordination.**
  - Collapse status into `coord.py status` values. Forbid per-commit announcement notes from sub-agents.
  - Replace "closure packet → other machine verifies" with a verifier both machines run locally on SHA manifests.
  - Main is red most days, with 545 test/ratchet-repair commits. Run check-main per commit in CI on one machine and auto-route failures, instead of messaging them.
  - Navigation documents are stale and huge: HANDOVER.md has 1,832 lines; plans/neuralese/HANDOVER.md opens with 10-03 to 10-09 streams describing discarded designs; POP_CURRENT.md was last reviewed 10-09 and describes the abandoned AR run. These cost every new session context and mislead it. Replace them with one short "current state" page per machine, regenerated, and delete the logs (storage order).
- **Storage.**
  - DGX `/` has 196G free and `/mnt/external` 571G; Pop has about 31 GiB free, below one 44 GB checkpoint.
  - Storage cost 101 commits and stalled training twice (HDD contention 10-12 08:38, disk reserve).
  - Delete the 57 stale recipes and their run directories. Bound checkpoint retention to the active lineage, and give Pop a hard checkpoint budget.

## 6. Ten recommendations, prioritized

1. **Put a natlang task-success eval in the loop, and debug LFM's 0/48 base first.**
   - *Impact:* the first metric of the actual target. It catches data and rendering bugs mid-run instead of after more than 10 days.
   - *First step:* treat 0/48 as a bug. Run the held-task suite on the base with the serving renderer against a teacher transcript of the same task, and localize where it diverges. Then add a 16-task subset to the cadence eval of both lines.
2. **Start a Neuralese E2E arm now from the current direct checkpoint, in parallel.**
   - *Impact:* the defining capability gets a learning signal two or more weeks earlier, and E2E bugs surface while SFT still runs.
   - *First step:* fill `line-parity.json` stages.e2e with a recipe that `extends` the shared base (MuonSR + drift monitor, unlike best225). Run it on Pop's LFM from its latest checkpoint, then on a Mellum v4 snapshot.
3. **Demote the generic behaviour gate to a report, per the owner's own breadth rule, and gate on natlang task success and drift.**
   - *Impact:* stops optimising against an arithmetic and stopping proxy.
   - *First step:* a DECISIONS entry. In `neuralese-direct-v2.json`, set the 48-probe gate to report-only once item 1's eval exists.
4. **Reinstate the nested family and full-ternary reach as in-stage objectives.**
   - *Impact:* restores two owner goals that were lost without a decision.
   - *First step:* record the current status in DECISIONS.
     - Add the member losses from `maple/nested_train.py` as an objective component in the shared recipe.
     - Rescale the QAT ramps to the stages that actually run, so that ternary-experts completes within v4 + trajectory.
     - Add the quantization component to LFM.
5. **Cut the cost per supervised token on the program-header phase.**
   - *Impact:* 766M tokens processed for 43M supervised. A 2× cut is likely worth days, more than further kernel work.
   - *First step:* profile the token mix per batch, then try prefix-shared batching or shorter header windows on the speed bench.
6. **Make the harness an on-policy bench.**
   - *Impact:* the harness becomes the training loop the vision describes and the source of RL/DPO data.
   - *First step:* run the student ± companion live matrix from HARNESS_BENCH §5 on 20 SWE/natlang tasks with the current LFM checkpoint, and record the results as a held cohort.
7. **Port our own operations policy to natlang.**
   - Scope: storage retention, data admission and pool approval, job scheduling and priority, gate explanation.
   - *Impact:* dogfoods the language on the busiest real program, produces trajectories, and aligns the project with its main rule.
   - *First step:* `storage_retention` classify as a pluggable `.nl` with a shadow mode against the crisp version for one week.
8. **Shrink coordination to state plus exact verifiers.**
   - *Impact:* frees agent time and context, roughly halving 450 messages per day.
   - *First step:*
     - Ban per-commit notes in `coord.py` (sub-agents post to status).
     - Replace closure requests with a shared `verify_manifest` both machines run.
     - Make one owner per lineage the sole announcer.
9. **Enforce one recipe lineage and delete discarded recipes, runs and stale handovers.**
   - *Impact:* removes misleading context, honours the storage order, and makes parity real.
   - *First step:*
     - A script listing the 57 stale recipes plus the roughly 40 superseded diagnostics, and their run directories. Delete them.
     - Rebase Pop's running recipe as an `extends` of `neuralese-direct-lfm-v3`.
     - Replace the HANDOVER and POP_CURRENT logs with short generated current-state pages.
10. **Restart the stalled natlang pillar work with a weekly slot.**
    - Scope: specializer on real apps, Tier 4, the measurement debt, the owner-review queue.
    - *Impact:* pillar 2 has been idle since 10-10, and 64 unmeasured prompt changes are accumulating risk to every live result.
    - *First step:*
      - Restart `natlang-specializer` with the pending dry run (memory specializer-loop) against nldb and pi.
      - Spend the next teacher window on the 64 MODEL_FACING_CHANGES rows (batched, 48 samples each).
      - Ask the owner to clear or bulk-accept the 29 OWNER_REVIEW items.
