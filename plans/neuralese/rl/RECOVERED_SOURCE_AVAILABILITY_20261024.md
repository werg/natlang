# Continuing recovered sources while retaining missing-data obligations

Policy: use every recovered original training source promptly. An unavailable
payload must remain an explicit obligation and must not force useful recovered
data to wait. Continue the earned model, optimizer, RNG and exact row visits.
This is temporary availability handling, not target withdrawal or permission to
claim full-recipe completion. Restore pending rows as soon as their bytes return.

The shared CE trainer accepts the recipe input `unavailable_native_sources`.
Its immutable `natlang.native-source-availability/1` declaration identifies the
model, stage, exact prior train source, unavailable leaf path/SHA/row counts and
the operational reason, with `preserve_obligations:true`. The mechanism derives
the affected row identities from the actual saved source tree and saved row
order. It checks every retained binding, refuses active/unknown/duplicated leaves
and permits only ordinary CE continuations. Normal source-order, protected-held,
model/optimizer identity and numerical/capacity checks remain in force.

Only the unavailable leaf rows leave the active binding/order view. Their exact
bindings, required visits and earned counts remain in the existing pending-source
ledger. The shared save/collect/readiness path keeps them incomplete, including
across restarts or flag omission. Restoring their exact bindings reactivates the
existing counts through the established append mechanism. Historical source
receipts and the bound availability declaration retain recovery provenance.

Validation includes actual tiny-model optimizer continuation after deleting a
payload and its old manifest, an idempotent restart, and subsequent restoration
and training of exactly the remaining row. Tests also check pending earned-credit
retention, unchanged optimizer/RNG/FP8 objects, absent-row readiness deficits and
rejection of altered source/stage/model/row authority. This mechanism is shared
by model lines; an availability declaration alone is not a deployed recipe.

Current recovery evidence:1,579,098 original LFM rows have local payloads, while
one7,027-row Search/OWL producer/consumer payload remains missing and had zero
earned visits. Sixty-six missing original manifest headers can be replaced by a
new explicit flattened manifest; they do not imply missing payloads. CPU source
preparation checks every available parsed row binding against the original earned
frame and warms the unchanged parser cache. Cutover still requires this complete
validation, original CE policy and held dependencies, actual graph capacity,
publication/export continuity and observed optimizer progress on genuine graphs.
