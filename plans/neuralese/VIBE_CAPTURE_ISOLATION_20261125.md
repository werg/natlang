# Preserve a wave when one artifact fails canonical parsing

The Source35 controller failed while processing an already captured wave because one structurally valid cross-task child graph met a stale same-task reader assumption. Reader parity is corrected independently; no original call/parent/task identity is rewritten. Artifact damage must also be isolated so that a genuinely malformed capture cannot prevent the other completed goals from being archived.

Inventory: `verified_episode` is the campaign import boundary shared by collection, seed loading, export and graph telemetry. Its parser can reject individual manifests, record bindings, schemas and child inventories; its subsequent teacher-model, held-closure and registered-task checks are campaign policy. `outcomes` is the collection receipt producer. Its existing `export-rejected` plus `capture_errors` category retains an invalid manifest and raw packet without admitting it; structural failures feed the existing wave hold and do not spend genuine goal retries. Export/telemetry/ingestion use `has_training_manifest`, so rejected artifacts never become training evidence. Seed and direct export callers still fail closed and preserve their complete evidence rather than masking policy errors.

The collection receipt producer now isolates parser/schema/file-closure errors per capture, retains the original paths/bytes and all other completed results, and propagates held/model/registered-task identity errors. Malformed raw gzip/JSON is preserved similarly. Interrupted damaged work stays interrupted and does not manufacture an infrastructure hold. No parse/graph/policy verification was weakened and no training verdict is invented. The actual valid cross-task capture is admitted only by the separately fixed reader.
