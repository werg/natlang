## 7. Known pitfalls (learned this session)

- `grep -l PATTERN /proc/*/cmdline` silently misses: scan /proc in Python for live references.
- A ledger queue head that cannot fit blocks smaller units behind it (no backfill beyond 0.5 GB): look at
  `memory_ledger.py status` `waiting[0].cannot_fit`.
- Collections on the HDD are many small files: moves run ~8 MB/s; a `du` of the destination can hang under contention.
- Other agents restart the aei1 driver (unit names like `aei1-drive-registry-recovered-*`, `-optional-return-*`,
  `-native-reference-*`) and add flags; always capture the live argv before restarting.
- `check-main.sh --quick` has a machine-paths ratchet: tests must not contain `/home/werg/...` literals.
