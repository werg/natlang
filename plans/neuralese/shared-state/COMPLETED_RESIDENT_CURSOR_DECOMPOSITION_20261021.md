# Completed adaptation and historical resident cursors

Policy: after an authenticated completed-operation continuation, retain its exact
historical cursor for accounting; use the new recipe for future work. A historical
resident generation cannot become evidence that the new recipe was trained.

1. The supervisor chooses a useful continuation in natural language.
2. Existing completed-operation verification authenticates the saved checkpoint,
   original controls, normal publication effects, preserved lineage and new recipe.
3. Crisp release plumbing identifies only the resident cursor in that verified
   operation's immutable published candidate (or raw completed candidate).
4. An exact match retains the historical cursor without replaying it as the new
   recipe. All other resident cursors still undergo normal append verification.
5. Foundation/task verifiers continue checking actual recipe/exposure. No new
   readiness, exposure or optimizer credit follows from this transport repair.

The crisp helper is an exact artifact-identity verifier, not a training policy.
Exercise a real resident append followed by a changed-recipe completed handoff,
reject mismatched historical cursors, and retain existing fresh-append coverage.
