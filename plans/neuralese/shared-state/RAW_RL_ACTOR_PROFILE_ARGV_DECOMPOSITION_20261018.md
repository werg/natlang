# Raw RL sampled profile and future ordinary continuation

Policy: train captured raw episodes with their authenticated actor history profile. A future ordinary recipe may opt into KEEP, but cannot reinterpret a previously sampled last-turn actor. Retain its checkpoint, captures, sidecar and failed admission receipt.

1. Derive RL argument values from the existing verified actor/tokenizer binding.
2. Override only the RL command's history policy and sidecar controls with that binding.
3. Keep ordinary seed arguments from the declared future recipe unchanged.
4. Reuse strict native-stage sidecar verification and existing command execution. No new inference, checkpoint load, model operation or admission waiver is introduced.

The argument builder is crisp mechanism: it binds already-decided policy to literal CLI values and prevents duplicate future flags from changing sampled-policy interpretation. Natural-language policy remains the owner-directed continuation above and the existing recipe.
