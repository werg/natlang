# Reuse verified positive sampled episodes without rewinding training

The declared policy is to train on correct TRAIN episodes which an earlier
GRPO operation omitted because their complete reward group had no variance.
Keep every occurrence and the original actor, immutable rollout, task/split,
repair, sampling and block-bundle provenance. These are old student samples,
not stronger-teacher generations or current-policy samples. Failed and unscored
episodes remain separate assigned obligations; do not turn them into positives.

The concrete first source is LF actor10407 (checkpoint0d781ea5), sealed797
collection, with eight correct no-variance episodes. Its original malformed
tool-history collection remains unchanged; only the independently bound actual
recording repair can be compiled. The current10659+ optimizer/RNG/exposure
must continue, without returning to10407.

1. Authenticate the complete original score, TRAIN case packet/current
   protection closure, exact captured actor and independently verified parent,
   original/repair join, all scored rollout occurrences and durable block
   bundle. Bind actual immutable paths rather than a moving monitoring alias.
2. Use existing `native_stage.prepare` / `native_replay.prepare_objective` RFT
   compilation to retain whole sampled episode graphs and sampled-action
   target masks. Compare the selected episode hashes against the actual prior
   GRPO objective; no previously used episode may be admitted again.
3. Require the declared eight episodes to be exact correct, scored TRAIN
   members of no-variance groups and absent from the old GRPO row inventory.
   Keep graph IDs equal to their original canonical sampled episode identities.
   Do not rename occurrences to hide duplication.
4. Inspect every compiled graph. A closed graph whose producer invocations are
   all present can become a positive ordinary-SFT source, preserving every node,
   position, mask, target weight and genuine edge. Do not carry an old captured
   external KV/hybrid state into the current model. An unresolved external
   dependency stays pending until its exact producer can be reconstructed by
   existing replay mechanisms; never flatten it or invent an observation.
5. Emit an immutable graph source and admission proof, recording the old actor
   separately from its future learner. Ordinary SFT is an explicit positive
   token objective, not a relabeled on-policy policy objective. The existing
   exact-actor NativePolicyObjective guard remains unchanged.

   the next real current checkpoint boundary through the existing source
   continuation. Preserve every prior optimizer value, RNG and per-row credit;
   these rows gain exposure only after actual recorded updates. Check complete
   graph/packed geometry against the current qualification before execution.

This numbered policy is natural language. Crisp code only verifies the exact
declared selection, immutable artifacts, complete graph/dependency closure and
durable output. Reuse existing RFT compilation, graph parsing, hashing,
protection and source-continuation mechanisms. No new sampler/model call,
optimizer objective arithmetic or live process is part of admission.

The first actual candidate was compiled on LF without constructing a model or
loading an optimizer checkpoint. All eight episodes are closed graphs (3, 16,
3, 5, 7, 20, 9 and 5 actual invocations) with 8,220 sampled actions. The exact
original mixed-group GRPO graph inventory was regenerated and matched the
saved objective. Current protection independently checked the two original
TRAIN task rows against held, quality, pending, decision and family holds.
Admission and exposure remain false until the source registration and owned
current-parent continuation are actually performed.

`native_positive_reuse.prepare_positive_reuse` is a crisp verifier/compiler
adapter for that declared selection. It does not choose new tasks, rewrite
rewards, run a model, grant readiness or mutate a policy objective. The future
ordinary SFT loss is deliberately ordinary positive-token CE; it is not the
old actor's temperature/masked policy loss. The sampled-action target mask and
all whole-graph contexts, producers, consumers and original occurrence IDs are
preserved. Old sampler details remain in the bound original RFT/rollout proofs.
