# Evaluate finite policy work without treating it as recipe completion

The curriculum admits complete policy episodes and the supervisor chooses their
declared evaluation cadence. Completing that finite policy operation does not
complete carried supervised sources or the whole training recipe.

1. Classify only the implemented nonmandatory native policy objective as a
   policy operation. Ordinary SFT and offline mandatory preference stages keep
   their existing full final held qualification.
2. For policy work, run the existing held selector with the declared budgets,
   whole-graph class minima and rotation even at the operation's final update.
   Report its real full/subset scope; do not call a subset full qualification.
3. Preserve authenticated held visit counters from the current checkpoint only
   when the held source SHA matches. Discard stale held losses and task proofs
   across changed-objective/stage restores while retaining those counters.
4. Report completed policy exposure independently of carried SFT deficits. Keep
   those exact deficits and all optimizer/RNG/exposure state. Policy completion
   never grants whole-SFT readiness.
5. Policy operations honor explicit task cadence, without a forced final task
   window. A finite supervised refresh alone also does not force final tasks;
   ordinary completion and genuine foundation qualification still do, and
   explicit positive task cadence remains active during updates.

These are exact scheduling/reporting mechanisms implementing existing declared
cadence. No budgets, model-facing text, source splits, model arithmetic, losses,
optimizer settings or graph geometry are changed.
