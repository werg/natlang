# Earned contracts and future operational tuning

Policy: acknowledge only the exact recipe stages executed by the drained owner. An already verified lineage handoff may tune operational parameters for future work without rewriting those earned contracts or granting credit for the new settings.

1. Verify the existing full owner, immutable parent, operation chain and recipe handoff.
2. Read its bound old and new plans. Require the requested future stages to match the verified new plan.
3. For different operational parameters, require identical ordered stage IDs/kinds and every source/input binding. The existing lineage_handoff verifier remains the authority for allowed parameter changes.
4. Check earned operation contracts and saved carried frames against the bound old stages. Keep the future recipe for subsequent execution; record both contracts and explicitly grant no future credit.

Crisp code authenticates exact artifacts and performs this durable reconciliation. It adds no training stage, source, exposure, optimizer update, readiness or numerical qualification.
